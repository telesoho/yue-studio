abc2svg
=======

Source: https://chiselapp.com/user/moinejf/repository/abc2svg
Version: 1.22.1
Copyright (C) 2014-2022 Jean-François Moine
License: LGPL-3.0+

Used by Yue Studio to render jianpu (numbered notation) when the user
clicks the "简谱" tab in the score viewer. abc2svg is invoked with
`%%jianpu true` on the voice to switch from Western staff notation to
JiǎnPǔ.

The original library ships as a single-file bundle that includes the ABC
parser, the SVG generation engine, and an embedded music font as base64.
No network fetch is performed at runtime; everything is served from this
directory.

If you upgrade abc2svg, remember:
- `%%jianpu` is voice-level and requires abc2svg >= 1.20.8.
- The bundle's interface suffix is `-1` (we instantiate
  `new abc2svg.Abc(user)`).
